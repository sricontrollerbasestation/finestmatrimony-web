Finest Refer & Earn — Referrer App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 23 October 2026  •  This policy explains how Referrer information is collected, used, protected, and retained when using the Referrer App.


1. Information We Collect

When you register for and use the Finest Refer & Earn Referrer App, we may collect information necessary to create and operate your Referrer account, manage referrals, calculate earnings, and process eligible payments.

  • Referrer name and registration details
  • Phone number and email address
  • Address and PIN code
  • Referrer ID and Referral Code
  • Bank name, branch, account number and IFSC
  • Referral and customer-related information submitted through the App
  • Earnings, payment and transaction records
  • Login, authentication and account activity information
  • Information you provide when contacting support

2. How We Use Referrer Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create, verify and manage your Referrer account.
  • Assign and manage your Referrer ID and Referral Code.
  • Record and validate referrals.
  • Calculate eligible referral earnings and maintain your passbook.
  • Process eligible payments and maintain payment records.
  • Communicate with you about your account, referrals, payments and services.
  • Provide technical and customer support.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Improve the Referrer App and related services.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory and accounting requirements.

3. Referrer ID & Referral Code

Each registered Referrer receives a unique Referrer ID. A Referral Code may be automatically assigned when the Referrer first registers.

Important: Your Referral Code is not necessarily permanent. The Sreebala™ may change, replace, deactivate, or reassign a Referral Code from time to time based on business, operational, security, or administrative requirements. The Referral Code currently assigned and recognized by the Sreebala™ will be treated as the valid Referral Code for referral activities.

4. Bank & Payment Information

Bank details provided by an Referrer are used to process eligible payments and maintain payment and accounting records. Referrers are responsible for providing accurate bank information. We take reasonable measures to protect payment-related information from unauthorized access or misuse.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent Referrer personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to operate the service, process payments, maintain security, or comply with applicable law, including with:

  • Payment and banking service providers
  • Hosting, cloud and technology service providers
  • Authentication and security service providers
  • Government, regulatory or law-enforcement authorities when legally required

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect Referrer information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or misuse. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Referrer information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain referral and payment records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal, tax, accounting or regulatory obligations.

8. Account Deletion

Referrers may request deletion of their Referrer App account and associated personal information by contacting us. Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal, accounting, payment, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate business purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The Finest Refer & Earn Referrer App is intended for adults and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Third-Party Services

The Referrer App may use third-party services for authentication, database storage, hosting, notifications, analytics, payment processing, security, or other operational purposes. Such providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices. Updated versions may be published through the Referrer App or our website. The effective date shown above will indicate the latest revision.

12. Contact & Privacy Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to raise a privacy concern, or wish to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, please contact us.
