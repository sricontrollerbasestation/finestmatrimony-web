How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Finest Matrimony account and personal data using the following methods.

Method 1: If You Can Login

  • Open the Finest Matrimony mobile application.
  • Login using your registered phone number. (if you are already login then not needed just open the app)
  • Go to the Accounts tab.
  • Open Settings.
  • Select Account Deletion.
  • Tap on Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion request.

Once confirmed, your account deletion request will be submitted successfully.

Method 2: If You Cannot Login

  • Send an email to our support team.
  • Use your registered phone number and account details in the email.
  • Request permanent account deletion.
Finest@sreebala.in

Account Deletion Processing Time

After confirmation, your account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Profile photographs
  • Phone number and contact details
  • Matrimony preferences
  • Messages and account-related data

Data Retention Policy

Certain information may be temporarily retained if required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security monitoring
  • Dispute resolution

Any retained information will be securely deleted after the legally required retention period.
