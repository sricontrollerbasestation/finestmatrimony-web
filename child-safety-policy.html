Child Safety Standards

Finest Matrimony is committed to creating a safe, secure, and respectful environment for all users.

Our Commitment to Safety

Finest Matrimony maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, harassment, and any harmful activity involving minors.

  • Finest Matrimony is strictly intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older.
  • Accounts found belonging to minors may be removed immediately.
  • Any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or exploitative content is strictly prohibited on our platform.
  • We actively review reports, suspicious activities, fake profiles, and abusive behavior to maintain platform safety.
  • Users who violate our safety standards may face account suspension or permanent removal.
  • We cooperate with applicable legal authorities and law enforcement agencies regarding serious safety concerns and child protection matters.

Reporting Safety Concerns

If you notice suspicious behavior, harassment, fake profiles, child exploitation content, or any other safety concern, please report it immediately.

How to Report:
  • Use the available reporting and blocking features inside the application.
  • Contact our dedicated child safety support team through email.
  • Share screenshots, profile details, or relevant information to help us investigate the issue quickly.

Content Moderation

Finest Matrimony uses moderation systems and manual review procedures to investigate reports related to child safety, fake accounts, abusive behavior, harassment, and harmful content.

Account Actions
